Four register classes, per the manual's diagrams. + − × ÷: X gets the result, Y takes Z, Z clears. yx: Y takes the old X, Z clears. sin cos tan ln log ex and inverses: Y holds, Z clears. 1/x √ x2 rad deg: Y and Z both hold. Nothing is ever replicated downward.

C↓ rolls the stack down — X discarded, Y→X, Z→Y, 0 into Z. Not a clear key. F then C↓ cancels the shift and changes nothing.

Lift is suppressed only right after ENT↑. π and MR lift unconditionally. Trig is always degrees; rad and deg convert X. An error zeroes X and Z, leaving Y and M intact.

Keyboard: digits, . + - * /, Enter, Backspace (C↓), f, c (CHS), x (x↔y), Esc.
